In short

Amaviro plays the TV service you already pay for. It doesn't have accounts, adverts or analytics. Your provider logins stay on your devices. Amaviro doesn't sell or share anything about you.

What Amaviro sends, and to whom

Your provider's servers, to sign in and play. ESPN's public feed, for fixtures and scores. Your own iCloud account, if you turn on sync or start a family. Apple, for purchases. No personal information goes anywhere else.

Deleting your information

Remove a provider in Settings, Your Providers, or use Sign Out on This Device to remove everything. Deleting the app deletes everything Amaviro stored on the device.

Terms of Use

Amaviro is a video player. It doesn't include, sell or host any channels, films, series or streams. You add a subscription you already have, and you're responsible for having the right to watch what it gives you. Apple's standard licence agreement also applies.
