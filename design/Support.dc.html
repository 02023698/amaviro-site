Something not working?

Start with Fix a Problem.

In the app: Settings, Fix a Problem. It checks your internet, your provider's server and the stream, and says what's wrong in plain English.

  1. 01A channel won't play. Press and hold the picture and choose Use Compatibility Player, or open Where to Watch for another channel.
  2. 02Nothing plays at all. Check the server line at the top of Now. "Not answering" is your provider's server; your provider can fix it.
  3. 03Your login stopped working. Settings, Your Providers, your line, Update Login. Paste your provider's new message.
  4. 04The guide is empty. Settings, Playlists and Guide, Refresh. With Plus, add a backup guide for the gaps.

TestFlight beta

Try what's next, and tell us what breaks.

Betas come through Apple's TestFlight app on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Every Plus feature is open while you test, and nothing you buy in a beta is charged. As a thank-you, testers get a founders’ code for Lifetime at £39.99 (usually £49.99) when Amaviro launches.

Join the Beta on TestFlight
How betas are numbered Each beta has a number: Beta 12 is version 0.9 (12) in TestFlight, the same on every device. Settings, About Amaviro shows yours. Please quote the beta number when you report something. Fixes arrive in a later beta.
Reporting a problem Take a screenshot in Amaviro and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. Say what you pressed, what you expected and what happened. Leave out your provider's login.
