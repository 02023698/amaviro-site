Questions

Before you start

More answers in Support
Does Amaviro come with channels?

No. Amaviro is a player. You add the subscription you already have from your TV provider: an Xtream login, an M3U playlist or a MAG portal.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad, Mac (Apple silicon) and Apple TV. Set up Apple TV from your iPhone in about a minute.

Is it free?

Watching is free: Live TV, the guide, films, series, sports and search. Amaviro Plus adds Fix It, more providers, Multiview with four screens, Family and more.

Where are my logins kept?

On your devices, in the Keychain. If you turn on iCloud sync, they go through iCloud Keychain, encrypted end to end. Amaviro has no servers that see them.

Amaviro

Amaviro is a video player. It doesn't include, sell or host any channels, films, series or streams, and isn't connected with any TV provider.
