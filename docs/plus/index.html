Good to know

How does the free trial work?

The Annual plan starts with 7 days free. If you turn it off before the 7 days end, you aren’t charged.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple Account settings, or from Amaviro: Settings, About Amaviro, Manage Subscription. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Can my family use it?

Yes. Plus works with Apple’s Family Sharing for up to five more people, and everyone in your Amaviro family gets it too.

I bought it on another device. How do I turn it on?

Open Settings, About Amaviro, Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

What is the founders’ price?

A thank-you to everyone who tested the beta: Lifetime for £39.99 instead of £49.99. Use the code from your beta invitation in Amaviro (Settings, About Amaviro, Redeem Code) or on the App Store. It works once per Apple Account.

Does Plus include channels?

No. Plus adds features to the player. Channels always come from your own TV provider.
