Support

How can we help?

Most problems are your provider's server or your connection. Amaviro can tell which, and what to do.

Setting up
01 Add your provider Paste the message your provider sent. Amaviro finds the server, username and password and fills in the rest. 02 Pick your sports Choose sports, leagues and teams once. Your matches then lead Now and Sports. 03 Set up Apple TV On your iPhone: Settings, Your Providers, Set Up Apple TV. Type the code your TV shows. 04 A new iPhone or iPad Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on. Amaviro offers to restore your providers.
Something not working?

Start with Fix a Problem.

In the app: Settings, Fix a Problem. It checks your internet, your provider's server and the stream, and says what's wrong in plain English.

  1. 01A channel won't play. Press and hold the picture and choose Use Compatibility Player, or open Where to Watch for another channel.
  2. 02Nothing plays at all. Check the server line at the top of Now. "Not answering" is your provider's server; your provider can fix it.
  3. 03Your login stopped working. Settings, Your Providers, your line, Update Login. Paste your provider's new message.
  4. 04The guide is empty. Settings, Playlists and Guide, Refresh. With Plus, add a backup guide for the gaps.
Questions
Where do I get a subscription?

From a TV provider of your choice. Amaviro doesn’t sell, recommend or connect you with providers.

Which login types work?

Xtream (server, username and password), M3U playlist links, and MAG portals with the MAC address your provider registered.

Why does a channel switch player?

Some streams use formats Apple’s player can’t open. Amaviro moves those to its Compatibility Player and remembers that for the channel.

My provider allows one stream. Can I use Multiview?

Amaviro tells you when a line is full and offers a swap. Some providers allow an extra stream from the same home; others stop one.

How do I share with my household?

Settings, Family (Amaviro Plus). Invite each person; they scan a code and their devices set themselves up.

How do I delete my data?

Remove a provider in Settings, Your Providers, or Sign Out on This Device to remove everything. Deleting the app removes it all too.

Contact Still stuck?

Email us from the app (Settings, About Amaviro, Contact Support) so your app version and device are included. We can't help with your provider's account or billing.
